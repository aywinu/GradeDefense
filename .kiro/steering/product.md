# Product: Deadline Defense — "Your semester is under siege."

A dark-fantasy tower-defense game where a student's real semester is the fortress and their coursework is the horde.

- Assignments → shambling enemies; quizzes → fast swarmers; exams → bosses; projects → multi-phase enemies.
- Deadline proximity sets enemy urgency/strength; neglected tasks grow more dangerous.
- Real progress powers combat: answering study-checkpoint questions (from course PDFs or the demo set) charges attacks; completing tasks earns upgrades.
- Never reward simply ticking "done" on real work. Rewards come from answering checkpoints correctly and from gameplay, so there's no incentive to fake progress.
- Must be fun with the bundled demo semester alone, with no login, Moodle, or paid AI.
- Onboarding is played, not read: first wave teaches the core loop in about 60 seconds.
- Category: Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest, so the UI must be haunting, polished, and memorable, and the spooky design has to serve the function.
