# Product: Deadline Defense — "Your semester is under siege."

A dark-fantasy tower-defense game where a student's real semester is the fortress and their coursework is the horde.

- Assignments → shambling enemies; quizzes → fast swarmers; exams → bosses; projects → multi-phase enemies.
- Deadline proximity sets enemy urgency/strength; neglected tasks grow more dangerous.
- Real progress powers combat: answering study-checkpoint questions (from course PDFs or the demo set) charges attacks; completing tasks earns upgrades.
- Never reward simply ticking "done" on real work. Rewards come from answering checkpoints correctly and from gameplay, so there's no incentive to fake progress.
- Must be fun with the bundled demo semester alone, with no login, Moodle, or paid AI.
- Onboarding is played, not read: first wave teaches the core loop in about 60 seconds.
- Category: Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest, so the UI must be haunting, polished, and memorable, and the spooky design has to serve the function.

## Useful first, game second (user direction, 2026-11-26)
This is a study tool wearing a costume, not a time-waster. A student who opens it must leave knowing where they stand and what to do next.
- **War Room** (home screen, before any battle): one card per course with the current grade, a threat level (from the priority score), and the next big enemies with real dates and weights ("Midterm Examination: Thu 3 Dec, 100 pts, in 4 days"). Also a semester timeline that shows every deadline as a tombstone on a calendar strip.
- **Battle plan**: for each upcoming big enemy, a short prep plan: which topics to review (from that course's checkpoint questions and, if loaded, its PDF), how many study sessions are left before the date, and a "Train now" button.
- **Weapons = studying.** Training means a 5-question practice trial for one course. Correct answers forge that course's tower upgrades and charge specials. Scores per course are tracked over time ("Linear Algebra readiness 60% → 80%"). Weak topics are shown so the student knows what to revisit.
- **Scout report** before each wave: "This week: Midterm (CV101), Quiz (PHO101). Your CV readiness is 40%. Train before the battle?"
- **After a battle**: a debrief that maps the game back to real life ("The Midterm boss broke through: your CV readiness is low. 2 trials recommended before Thursday").
- The battle stays fun and short (a wave is 1–3 minutes), but every screen points back to the real plan.
